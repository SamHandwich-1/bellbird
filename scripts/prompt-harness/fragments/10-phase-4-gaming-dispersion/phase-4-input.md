## Adversarial review (Phase 3)

**contrarian_argument**

The thesis treats Roblox and Take-Two as direct substitutes whose revenues move inversely with under-25 engagement hours, but the data show limited wallet overlap — Roblox monetizes primarily through small, frequent microtransactions from users who rarely buy $60–70 AAA titles, while Take-Two's core franchises draw from a higher-ARPU cohort that also spends on live-service games without reducing Roblox time. The mechanism therefore requires not just engagement dispersion but actual revenue substitution, which has not appeared in either company's cohort-level metrics or in historical console/PC versus mobile/UGC spending patterns. A more direct risk is that Roblox's growth slows from creator monetization compression and regulatory limits on child-directed virtual economies, while Take-Two's next major release cycle resets multiples without any corresponding loss of "gas."
